Privacy policy

for the app Nukidos – German for Kids. Last updated: 2026-09-30

In short

Nukidos does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking, no account and no internet access.

Controller

Deutschland
Email: support@bouzekri.de

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so your child can repeat a word and then hear themselves. The recording (three seconds) is only played back on the device and deleted right after. It is not stored, not sent anywhere and not analysed – there is no speech recognition. Without this access the app keeps working, just without the microphone buttons.

Learning progress

Which words have been practised and how hard the games are is stored only on the device. It never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Purchase through Google Play

Google handles the purchase; Google's privacy policy applies to it. For a purchase, Google only passes on to us what is needed to process it (for example the order number and country), and we use it for nothing else.

Crash reports

The app itself sends no reports. Google Play may collect crash and performance data if this is allowed in the device's settings; we only see it aggregated in the Google Play Console.

Children

Nukidos is made for children aged 3 to 6. Because the app collects no personal data, no data is collected from children either.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Since we store no data about you, there is usually nothing to disclose – but feel free to write to us: support@bouzekri.de.
