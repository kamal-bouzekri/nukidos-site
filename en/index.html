Nukidos teaches German to children aged 3 to 6 the way children pick up a language: by listening, repeating and playing. The app speaks only German – no translations, just like growing up with the language. WHAT YOUR CHILD DISCOVERS • 7 topics with 148 words: animals, colours, food & kitchen, body & clothes, shapes, numbers 1 to 10 and the letters A to Z with Ä, Ö and Ü • Every word with a picture and a clear narrator voice – nouns always with their article ("der Hund", "die Katze") • Whole sentences, not just words: "Das ist der Hund. Der Hund macht Wuff!" PLAY AND REPEAT • Discover: look at each word, hear it and say it yourself • "Wo ist …?" (Where is …?): find the right picture • Memory, popping balloons, "Gib mir …" (Give me …), sorting, colouring and painting • 5 sing-along songs – the pictures appear just as each word is sung SPEAK FOR YOURSELF Your child taps the microphone, says the word and then hears themselves. The recording stays on the device and is deleted right away.
